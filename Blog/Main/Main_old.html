🧭 What We Cover

ShipPaulJobs is a practitioner-led maritime cybersecurity and compliance platform focused on IACS UR E26 and E27 implementation, OT system security, and AI-driven risk management for ships and offshore assets. Content is written by working consultants with direct experience in class approval, vessel commissioning, and flag state compliance — not academic theory. The platform covers network segmentation under IEC 62443, zone-and-conduit architecture for shipboard systems, and the practical gap between what classification societies require and what shipyards deliver.

IACS UR E26 / E27

IACS Unified Requirements E26 and E27 mandate cyber resilience from contract signing after 1 July 2024. UR E26 defines minimum requirements for network architecture, access control, software update procedures, and incident response. UR E27 covers supply chain cyber security documentation per equipment supplier. Coverage includes clause-by-clause IEC 62443 cross-references and deliverable guidance for class surveyors and flag state inspectors.

Maritime OT Security

Operational Technology on modern vessels — integrated bridge systems, propulsion control, ballast management, cargo handling automation, dynamic positioning — is increasingly networked and cyber-exposed. Unlike IT, maritime OT has long lifecycles and safety-critical real-time constraints. This platform provides OT-specific guidance on asset inventory, vulnerability analysis, and defence-in-depth strategy aligned with IMO MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3 and BIMCO recommendations.

AI in Maritime Operations

AI is reshaping fleet management, predictive maintenance, port logistics, and compliance monitoring. This platform examines how LLMs, autonomous agents, and structured maritime data — AIS feeds, class records, inspection histories — reduce compliance burden and increase operational visibility. Coverage targets ship operators, class surveyors, shipyard project managers, and flag state administrators who need AI insight without vendor-driven hype.

Industry Trends, Talent & Careers

The maritime industry is undergoing structural transformation driven by decarbonisation, digitalisation, and tightening cyber regulation. This platform discusses the evolving competency requirements for seafarers, shore-based engineers, compliance officers, and surveyors — and how the hiring market is responding. Topics include cross-disciplinary skills at the intersection of OT engineering and cyber security, the emergence of maritime cyber roles at flag states and classification societies, salary benchmarks, and practical pathways for professionals transitioning into this space. A community-oriented space where industry practitioners share insights and shape the conversation on maritime's digital future.
