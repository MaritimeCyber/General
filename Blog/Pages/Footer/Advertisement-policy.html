Pricing Policy

All prices are monthly-based and subject to change depending on the waiting queue and competition ranking for each ad position. Higher demand for a position may result in adjusted pricing or a waitlist. Current rates are confirmed at the time of inquiry.

  • Billing cycle: Calendar month — 1st to last day of each month. Standard placements begin on the 1st of the following month. For immediate go-live, the full calendar month fee applies including days elapsed since the 1st.
  • Position displacement: When a new higher-priority booking is confirmed for the same position, the existing advertiser is notified at least 7 days in advance. A prorated refund for the remaining period is issued, or the advertiser may opt to move to an equivalent available position.

📋 Terms & Notes
  • Billing period: Calendar month basis — from the 1st to the last day of each month. One calendar month = one billing unit regardless of number of days in that month.
  • Start date — standard: Placements begin on the 1st of the month. Inquiries confirmed after the 3rd take effect from the 1st of the following month at the standard monthly rate.
  • Immediate placement: Advertisers who request placement to go live before the 1st of the following month are charged the full calendar month fee — including days already elapsed since the 1st of the current month. Example: if confirmed on the 20th, the fee covers the 1st through the last day of that month in full (no proration).
  • Position queue & displacement: Positions are allocated on a first-confirmed, highest-priority basis. If a new advertiser books a currently occupied position at a higher tier or longer term, the existing advertiser receives 7 days written notice and a prorated refund for the remaining days, or the option to transfer to an equivalent available position.
  • Price changes: Listed prices may increase as demand for a position rises. The rate confirmed at the time of booking is locked for that term.
  • Prices are listed in USD. Korean Won (KRW) equivalents available on request.
  • All prices are net — no additional platform fees.
  • Sponsored articles are written collaboratively; final content approved by both parties.
  • Job postings must be for genuine open roles in maritime AI, data, or cybersecurity.
  • ④ Job Posting billing: Priced per posting period (30 / 60 / 90 days) and exempt from the calendar month cycle. The posting period begins on the confirmed go-live date regardless of calendar position.
  • Discounts apply for long-duration bookings; custom packages available for multi-position campaigns.
