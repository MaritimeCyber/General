ShipPaulJobs is committed to delivering reliable maritime intelligence through a structured editorial and technical verification process. This policy explains how articles are reviewed, verified, and kept up to date.

Our Commitment

ShipPaulJobs is an independent global maritime knowledge platform dedicated to publishing reliable, technically accurate, and practical insights on maritime cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, OT/ICS security, digital transformation, and international maritime regulations.

Our editorial process is designed to ensure that every published article meets high standards of technical quality, transparency, and professional integrity.

Through collaboration with contributors, technical advisors, and maritime industry experts, we strive to provide trusted knowledge that supports informed decision-making across the global maritime community.

Editorial Principles

Every article published on ShipPaulJobs follows these principles:

✔
Accuracy — Information is verified using reliable technical references whenever possible.
✔
Technical Integrity — Articles are reviewed to ensure consistency with current engineering practices and maritime standards.
✔
Editorial Independence — Editorial decisions are made independently and objectively.
✔
Practical Value — Content is intended to support real-world maritime operations and decision-making.
✔
Transparency — Verification status is clearly communicated through editorial badges.
✔
Continuous Improvement — Articles are periodically updated as regulations, technologies, and industry practices evolve.

Content Contributors

ShipPaulJobs welcomes contributions from qualified professionals, including:

ShipPaulJobs Editorial Team Guest Authors Maritime Industry Experts Researchers Technical Advisors Marine Surveyors Shipbuilders Equipment Suppliers Classification Society Professionals
All submissions undergo editorial assessment before publication. Submission does not guarantee publication.

Editorial & Verification Workflow

Every published article follows a structured, four-stage review process.

Stage 1
Author Submission

Articles are prepared by the original author or invited contributor. The Editorial Team conducts an initial assessment covering:

Relevance Originality Technical scope Readability Reference quality
Stage 2
Editorial Review

The ShipPaulJobs Editorial Team reviews:

Editorial quality Technical consistency Reference verification Clarity & readability Publication standards
🟢 VERIFIED Reviewed, fact-checked, and approved by the ShipPaulJobs Editorial Team.
Stage 3
Expert Review

Selected technical articles undergo additional review by independent subject matter experts, who may include:

Maritime Cybersecurity Specialists OT / ICS Security Experts AI Specialists Researchers Technical Advisors
🔵 EXPERT REVIEWED Technically reviewed by one or more independent subject matter experts.
Stage 4
Industry Validation

For operational guidance, implementation practices, and regulatory interpretation, ShipPaulJobs may incorporate practical feedback from experienced maritime professionals, including:

Shipowners Shipyards Equipment Suppliers Classification Societies Marine Consultants System Integrators
🟣 INDUSTRY VALIDATED Reviewed with practical feedback from experienced maritime industry professionals.

Verification Badge

Articles that complete a verification stage receive a circular badge — the ShipPaulJobs emblem framed by a color-coded ring — displayed next to the share button on every post card and post detail page.

Level 1 — Verified
Reviewed, fact-checked, and approved by the ShipPaulJobs Editorial Team. Meets editorial quality, technical consistency, and reference verification standards.
Level 2 — Expert Reviewed
Technically reviewed by one or more independent subject matter experts from the Technical Advisory Board, including maritime cybersecurity specialists, OT/ICS experts, and AI researchers.
Level 3 — Industry Validated
Reviewed with practical operational feedback from experienced maritime professionals including shipyards, classification societies, equipment suppliers, and marine consultants.
Where Badges Appear
▸
Post Card View — The badge icon appears to the left of the share button (⬤ green circle) in the upper-right corner of every post card.
▸
Post Detail View — The badge icon and its text label (e.g. Verified, Expert Reviewed) appear to the left of the share button at the top-right of the post.
▸
Clicking the Badge — Links to this Editorial & Verification Policy page for full context.

Article Updates

ShipPaulJobs is committed to maintaining accurate and current technical information. Articles may be revised when:

•
International regulations are updated
•
Technical standards change
•
New cybersecurity threats emerge
•
Industry practices evolve
•
Errors are identified
•
Additional expert feedback becomes available

For significant revisions, the article's review date and version history may also be updated.

Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions are made independently by ShipPaulJobs. Technical reviews and industry feedback are intended solely to improve the accuracy and quality of published content.

Verification badges do not imply endorsement by any individual, company, shipyard, equipment supplier, classification society, government authority, or international organization.

Corrections Policy

Maintaining trust requires continuous improvement. If factual inaccuracies are identified, ShipPaulJobs will review the information and, where appropriate:

•
Correct technical errors
•
Update outdated information
•
Clarify ambiguous statements
•
Publish revised versions when necessary

Readers are encouraged to contact the Editorial Team if they discover inaccuracies or wish to provide constructive technical feedback.

Disclaimer

The information published on ShipPaulJobs is provided for educational, informational, and professional knowledge-sharing purposes only.

Although every effort is made to ensure technical accuracy, readers should always consult the latest official publications issued by relevant authorities, classification societies, equipment manufacturers, and international organizations before making operational, regulatory, engineering, or commercial decisions.
