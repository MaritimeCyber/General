About This Platform

ShipPaulJobs

AI · Data · Cybersecurity for the Maritime Industry

Who We Are

ShipPaulJobs (SPJ) is an independent research and intelligence platform dedicated to shaping the future of the maritime industry through the convergence of Artificial Intelligence, data analytics, and cybersecurity.

Founded by maritime professionals with hands-on experience in shipbuilding, OT/IT security, and maritime compliance, SPJ bridges the gap between traditional maritime operations and emerging digital technologies — covering smart ship regulations such as IACS UR E26 & E27, AI-driven operations, and real-time threat intelligence.

What We Cover

🤖
AI & Data
Smart ship technology, predictive maintenance, AI-driven route optimization, maritime data engineering
🔐
Cyber & OT Security
IACS UR E26/E27, OT/IT threats, GPS jamming, AIS spoofing, ransomware targeting ports
📋
Compliance & Regulation
IMO resolutions, IACS unified requirements, port state control, cyber risk management frameworks
🚢
Naval & Digital
Global newbuilding order trends, shipyard analysis, digital twin, autonomous vessel technology
💼
Maritime Jobs & Talent Intelligence
Real-time global job trends in AI, Data & Cyber for the maritime sector — aggregated from LinkedIn, Indeed, and major maritime employers worldwide
