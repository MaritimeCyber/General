Who We Are

ShipPaulJobs (SPJ) is an independent research and intelligence platform dedicated to shaping the future of the maritime industry through the convergence of Artificial Intelligence, data analytics, and cybersecurity.

Founded by maritime professionals with hands-on experience in shipbuilding, OT/IT security, and maritime compliance, SPJ bridges the gap between traditional maritime operations and emerging digital technologies — covering smart ship regulations such as IACS UR E26 & E27, AI-driven operations, and real-time threat intelligence.
