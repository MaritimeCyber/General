IACS UR E26 & E27 are the unified regulations on ship cyber resilience, mandatory for newbuildings contracted on or after 1 July 2024. Each of the 9 IACS member class societies publishes its own interpretation guidelines, reflecting national industry characteristics, shipyard collaboration structures, and regulatory environments. This page lets you review each society's profile and download their official documents at a glance.

📋 IACS UR E26
Cyber Resilience of Ships
●Scope: Newbuildings contracted on/after 1 Jul 2024
●Coverage: All OT/IT systems on board (ship level)
●Core: Identify · Protect · Detect · Respond · Recover (5 functions)
●Design: Security Zone & Conduit architecture
🔧 IACS UR E27
Cyber Resilience of Equipment
●Scope: Individual equipment installed on E26 ships
●Coverage: OEM equipment manufacturers (equipment level)
●Core: CAL (Cyber Approval Level) 1–4 assessment
●Design: Secure by Design principle at product level
