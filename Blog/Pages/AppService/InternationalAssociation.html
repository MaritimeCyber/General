Maritime cyber compliance draws from a layered regulatory ecosystem — IACS unified requirements binding on newbuildings, BIMCO/ICS industry guidelines for operations, NIST and IEC technical frameworks for security architecture, and EU legislation such as NIS2 and GDPR shaping shore-side obligations. This page lets you browse each body's profile and download their official documents at a glance.

⚓ IACS UR E26/E27
Mandatory Newbuilding Standard
●Scope: Newbuildings contracted on/after 1 Jul 2024
●UR E26: Ship-level cyber resilience (OT/IT systems)
●UR E27: Equipment-level CAL 1–4 assessment (OEM)
●Enforced by: All 9 IACS member class societies
📋 BIMCO Guidelines v5
Industry Operational Standard
●Published: November 2024 (v5, latest edition)
●Authors: BIMCO, ICS, INTERCARGO, INTERTANKO, OCIMF
●Framework: NIST CSF · IMO MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3 Rev.2 aligned
●Scope: OT/IT operations, crew, incident response, supply chain
