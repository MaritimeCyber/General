Frequently Asked Questions

What topics does ShipPaulJobs cover?

ShipPaulJobs publishes articles across six core areas: maritime cybersecurity (IACS UR E26/E27, OT security, vessel pen testing), ship systems and technology (IBS, ECDIS, engine automation, onboard networks), marine solutions and services (performance platforms, digital twins, connectivity), maritime careers and job intelligence, regulatory compliance (IMO, MARPOL, CII/EEXI), and maritime industry news and analysis. All articles are written or reviewed by maritime professionals.

How do the topic filters work?

Each topic tab (Ship Systems, Marine Solutions, Cybersecurity, Maritime AI, Regulations, Digital, News) filters the post archive by the matching Blogger label. Posts with multiple labels may appear under more than one topic. The "All" tab shows every published post. Use the search bar to further narrow results within any topic view.

What does the "Popular" sort show?

The Popular sort reorders visible posts by their view count, fetched live from the site's page-visit counter (Abacus tracker, deployed site-wide). Top-viewed posts appear first regardless of publication date. Switch back to Latest to restore chronological order. The Top Ranked section at the bottom always shows the globally most-viewed posts across the entire archive.
