AIS Live Intelligence

ShipPaulJobs Vessel & Fleet Analysis integrates live AIS (Automatic Identification System) data with fleet management analytics, providing maritime professionals with a real-time operational picture of vessel movements, port activity, and traffic density worldwide. AIS data is the backbone of modern maritime domain awareness, mandated under SOLAS Chapter V for vessels over 300 GT on international voyages.

This tool is designed for ship operators monitoring fleet positions, port state control officers assessing inbound traffic, freight forwarders tracking cargo vessel ETAs, and maritime security professionals evaluating exposure to high-risk transit areas. The integrated map supports zoom-to-vessel inspection, voyage history, and route deviation detection — capabilities directly relevant to situational awareness requirements under IMO MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3 and BIMCO Cybersecurity Guidelines v5.

All AIS data is sourced from VesselFinder and updated in real time. Search by vessel name, MMSI, port, country, or coordinates. No account registration required for basic tracking functionality.
