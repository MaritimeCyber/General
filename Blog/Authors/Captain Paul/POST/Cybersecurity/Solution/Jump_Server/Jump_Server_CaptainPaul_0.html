Series Purpose

This series explains why jump servers are a foundational component of secure remote access architecture for modern vessels. It addresses the engineering rationale, design principles, evaluation criteria, and regulatory alignment required for Cyber-Enabled Systems (CBS) aboard smart ships.

This is not a product review. It is a technical education series for engineers, naval architects, shipyard professionals, classification society surveyors, and cybersecurity practitioners working in the maritime domain.
